# Incident Response

Severity 1 includes confirmed cross-tenant access, credential exposure, destructive data loss, or material service compromise. The on-call owner must contain access, preserve evidence, rotate affected secrets, assess data and tenants, and open an incident log immediately. Notify affected controllers without undue delay and within contractual deadlines; do not speculate before facts are validated.

Sequence: detect and timestamp; assign incident commander; contain; preserve logs and image/database copies; determine root cause and affected records; remediate; verify clean service; notify; monitor; publish an internal post-incident review within five business days. Maintain current emergency contacts, cloud escalation paths, counsel, insurer, and customer notification templates outside the repository.
