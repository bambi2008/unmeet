# Support and Service Boundaries

Initial support is email-based on business days. Target first responses: Severity 1 within 4 business hours, blocked workflow within 1 business day, general requests within 2 business days. This is a target, not an SLA unless included in an order form.

Supported: account access, CSV/Tencent imports, Google/Microsoft calendar connection, role management, export/deletion, and verified-results workflow. Not yet supported: online card payment, enterprise SSO/SCIM, active-active hosting, transcript analysis, or guaranteed platform-wide calendar discovery without customer admin authorization.

Support staff must verify requester identity and workspace authority, avoid receiving secrets or full customer exports by email, and record reproducible steps with redacted identifiers.
