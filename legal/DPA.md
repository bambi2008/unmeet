# Data Processing Addendum Template

The customer is controller and the UnMeet operator is processor for workspace data. Processing covers account administration, calendar metadata import, recurring-meeting analysis, owner decisions, verification, support, security logging, export, and deletion for the subscription term plus the documented backup-rotation period.

The processor will: follow documented instructions; bind personnel to confidentiality; maintain access control, encryption in transit, encrypted OAuth secrets, backups, monitoring, and incident procedures; use approved subprocessors; assist with data-subject requests and impact assessments; notify the customer without undue delay after confirming a personal-data breach; delete or return customer data at termination; and provide reasonable compliance evidence.

International transfers require the applicable SCCs or other valid mechanism. The parties must complete controller/processor legal names, contacts, hosting region, categories of data subjects, security schedule, breach-notice deadline, audit terms, and governing law before signature. Counsel approval is an external launch gate.
