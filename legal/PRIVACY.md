# UnMeet Privacy Notice

Effective: 13 August 2026

UnMeet processes account details, workspace membership, calendar event metadata, meeting decisions, usage records, and security/audit events to provide meeting-governance services. The product does not require meeting audio, video, chat, transcripts, or message content.

The customer is the controller of imported workforce and calendar data; the UnMeet operator is its processor. Account and security data are also processed to provide, protect, and comply with the service. Data is used only for service delivery, security, support, and legally required compliance—not for advertising or selling personal data.

Calendar access is read-only and limited to the connected user's events. OAuth credentials are encrypted using AES-256-GCM. Customers can export workspace data and permanently delete a workspace. Production backups expire within 30 days; deletion therefore propagates to backup rotation within 30 days. Minimal hashed deletion receipts are retained to demonstrate fulfillment.

Data may be processed by the hosting, email, monitoring, and calendar providers listed in [SUBPROCESSORS.md](./SUBPROCESSORS.md). International transfers must use the contractual safeguards applicable to the customer's region. Requests for access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability should be sent to the support/privacy address configured by the operator. Identity will be verified before disclosure or deletion.

The production operator must replace the contact and legal-entity placeholders in the deployment record before launch. This notice is an operational template and must receive counsel review for the operator's jurisdictions.
