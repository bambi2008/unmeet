Privacy, terms and security

Privacy

UnMeet processes account details, workspace memberships, calendar event metadata, meeting decisions and security events to provide meeting governance. It does not require meeting audio, video, chat or transcripts, and does not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Calendar access is read-only. OAuth credentials are encrypted. Workspace administrators can export data and permanently delete live workspace records; encrypted backups expire within 30 days. Contact the operator's published privacy address for access, correction, deletion or portability requests.

Service terms

Customers must have a lawful basis and provide required workforce notices before importing employee or calendar data. UnMeet must not be used for solely automated employment decisions. Online payment is not enabled; commercial terms are agreed through an order form.

Security and retention

  • Seven-day absolute and twelve-hour idle session limits.
  • Role-based tenant isolation and recent password confirmation for sensitive operations.
  • Encrypted OAuth secrets, HTTPS-only production cookies and restrictive browser headers.
  • Live workspace deletion is immediate; backups are retained for no more than 30 days.
  • Security incidents follow a documented containment, evidence, notification and recovery process.

The production operator must publish its legal entity and privacy/security/support contacts. Full contractual documents are supplied with the customer order form.
