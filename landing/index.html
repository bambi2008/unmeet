How the reset works

From calendar clutter to verified capacity in 30 days.

Every insight has an accountable owner, a concrete decision, and a date when the result should be checked.

1

Establish the baseline

Import Google Calendar metadata and rank recurring series by monthly person-hours—not an opaque “health score.”

2

Make owners decide

Keep, shorten, reduce cadence, remove attendees, move async, or cancel. Every decision gets an effective date and review date.

3

Verify what changed

Compare the next calendar period with the baseline. Planned savings stay separate from observed results.

Result integrity

We do not turn recommendations into fake ROI.

A suggested change is not a saving. UnMeet only reports recovered capacity after the new meeting pattern appears in calendar metadata.
